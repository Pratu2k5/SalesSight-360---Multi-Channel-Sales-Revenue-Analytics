# 📊 SalesSight 360 - Multi-Channel Sales & Revenue Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

SalesSight 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze
multi-channel sales performance, revenue, profit, forecasting, sales
funnel performance, products, customers, regions, and payment status.

The project transforms sales data into interactive visualizations and
business insights to support data-driven decision making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Track important business KPIs
- Compare revenue across states and channels
- Analyze sales funnel and forecast performance
- Identify high-performing products and customers
- Analyze payment status and sales stages
- Monitor regional and product performance

## 📊 Key KPIs

- Total Revenue: ₹150M
- Total Orders: 3K
- Total Profit: ₹40.71M
- Forecast Value: ₹71.97M
- Average Order Value: ₹50K
- Win Rate: 70%

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Maharashtra generated the highest state-wise revenue.
- Direct Sales contributed the highest channel revenue.
- Electronics generated the highest profit among product categories.
- Laptop has the highest forecast value among products.
- Closed Won represents the largest sales stage.
- The dashboard highlights a significant gap between target revenue
  and forecast value.

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview

Provides an executive-level view of:
- Revenue
- Orders
- Profit
- Forecast Value
- Average Order Value
- Win Rate
- Revenue by State
- Revenue by Channel
- Monthly Revenue vs Forecast
- Profit by Product Category

### 2. Sales Funnel & Forecast Analysis

Includes:
- Target Revenue
- Forecast Value
- Forecast Achievement %
- Forecast Variance
- Forecast by Region
- Closed Won Deals by Lead Source
- Target vs Forecast by Month
- Sales Records by Current Stage

### 3. Product & Sales Performance

Includes:
- Sales Records by State
- Orders by Payment Status
- Top 10 Customers by Forecast Value
- Forecast Value by Product
- Product and Sales Performance Analysis
